## Behaviour assumptions

Behaviour comes from circumstances only. Every effect is an assumption.

- **price sensitivity**: income Q1 x1.25, Q2 x1.12, Q3 x1.00, Q4 x0.92, Q5 x0.85. Why: Less money left over means a price rise is felt more. Direction is well established; the size is ours.
- **price sensitivity**: lone parent x1.08, couple with children x1.05, unemployed or student x1.05. Why: More people to pay for, or an income gap, narrows the budget further.
- **patience**: retired x1.15, works from home x1.05, employed (not at home) x0.95. Why: Patience here is how long someone waits for an answer. Time at home and no deadline means longer; a day at work means shorter.
- **patience**: new parent x0.85, carer x0.9. Why: Little spare time and attention. Assumption.
- **buying rate**: online shopping: never x0.5, rarely x0.8, monthly x1.0, weekly or more x1.3. Why: Someone who rarely buys online buys from an online shop less often. Mild on purpose.
- **channel**: the preferred channel trait (email, chat, phone) passes straight through. Why: Drawn by age from channel table; older people lean to the phone.
- **contact time**: working away from home: 18:00 to 22:00; works from home: 09:00 to 17:00; retired: 09:00 to 13:00; student: 20:00 to 24:00; new parent: 20:00 to 23:00; otherwise 10:00 to 16:00. Why: People write when they have a spare moment, which follows their day.
- **category demand**: new parent: everyday x1.3, food x1.15; moving home: desk-home x1.4, everyday x1.2; carer: everyday x1.1, food x1.1; student: tech x1.2, desk-home x1.2, carry x1.2; new job: carry x1.3, desk-home x1.15; recently retired: outdoors x1.2, food x1.1; works from home: desk-home x1.35, tech x1.15; keen gardener: outdoors x1.5. Why: What a life event makes people need. Sizes are ours; categories are the shop's (carry, desk-home, everyday, food, outdoors, tech).

## Limits

- Household, employment, online and channel are each dealt by age; they are independent of each other given age (a lone parent is not made likelier to be unemployed). Income links to employment and household.
- Circumstance prevalences are assumptions. Ties come from book and address.
- Fairness checks need at least 30 people in a group (outcomesByGroup says "too few to judge" otherwise).
