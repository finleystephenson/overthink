# Complaints

## The short version

If something has gone wrong and you are unhappy, tell us. A person looks at it. We reply fast. If we cannot agree, you can go elsewhere at any time.

## How do I complain?

Ask us through the website, 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Saturday. Or tell the assistant you want to complain. Give your order, booking or repair number and what you would like us to do.

## What happens next?

- **Stage 1:** a member of the support team looks at it. We tell you we have it within 1 working day. We answer within 3 working days.
- **Stage 2:** if you are not happy, ask for stage 2. The Support Lead looks at it again and answers within 10 working days. This is our final answer.

You complain about a late parcel on Monday. We reply by Tuesday. You have our answer by Thursday.

## What does the assistant do?

If you tell the assistant you are unhappy, it writes it down and passes it to a person. It cannot make exceptions or give goodwill gifts.

## What if it is about injury, allergy or the law?

These go straight to a person. This includes injury, an allergic reaction, a safety worry or a legal threat.

## What can we offer?

Depending on the problem, we may repair, replace, refund or explain. A support agent can give a goodwill credit of up to £15. The Support Lead can make an exception to a policy and must write down why.

## Does my complaint limit my legal rights?

No. Making a complaint does not stop you using your legal rights. You can still ask for a repair, replacement or refund, or go to court. The time limit for a legal claim is 6 years (5 in Scotland).

## Is there anyone outside the company?

We are not a member of an ombudsman or other dispute resolution scheme. We have not agreed to use one. You can still:

- get free advice from Citizens Advice (citizensadvice.org.uk)
- tell Trading Standards
- make a claim in the small claims court
- if you paid by card, ask your card company about a chargeback

## Working days

Monday to Friday, not bank holidays.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
