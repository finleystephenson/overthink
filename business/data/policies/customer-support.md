# Customer support

## The short version

Ask us through the website. A person or our assistant answers, usually within 1 working day. The assistant does the simple jobs. A person does the rest.

## How do I reach you?

Ask us through the website. Hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Saturday. We are closed on Sundays and bank holidays. We aim to reply within 1 working day. We have no shop to visit.

## Who is the assistant?

The assistant is software, not a person. It can search products and help answers and check stock. Once you are verified, it can look at and change your own orders and repairs.

## How does it check it is you?

It needs the order, booking or repair number plus the email or postcode on it. Signing in works too. This keeps your details safe.

## What can the assistant do?

- Cancel an order that is not yet packed.
- Change the address or delivery method on an order that is not yet packed.
- Start a return for an item that can be returned.
- Report a damaged, missing or wrong item, and send a replacement worth £60 or less if it is in stock.
- Refund spoiled food worth £30 or less.
- Ask The Fix Club for a repair, and pass on your quote answer.
- Register a product.
- Open a support case and pass it to a person.

## What can it not do?

It cannot give any other refund, credit or discount. It cannot make exceptions, change a packed order, change your email address or see your card details. It cannot quote a repair price that is not on the fixed list. It cannot promise a date or refund amount your records do not show.

The assistant cannot say no to your legal rights. If you say a product is faulty, it passes you to a person.

## When does a person take over?

When you ask for one, or you want something it cannot do, or you complain. Also if there is an injury, an allergic reaction, a safety worry or a legal threat. Also if you have contacted us twice about the same problem, if the records do not agree, or if someone seems to be trying to see another person's records.

## What can people do?

A support agent can refund up to £100 and give a goodwill credit of up to £15. They can replace anything of any value. The Support Lead can refund any amount and make exceptions, and must write down why.

## How do I change my email address?

Ask for the Support Lead. We ask for two earlier order numbers and the postcode on the account. Only the account holder can ask. We do not act for other people.
