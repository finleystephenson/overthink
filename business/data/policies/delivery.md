# Delivery

## The short version

We deliver to the UK mainland. Pick Soon, Sooner or Cold. The price is shown before you pay. Until the parcel is in your hands, it is our problem if it goes wrong.

## Where do you deliver?

The UK mainland only. We do not deliver to Northern Ireland, the Scottish Highlands and Islands, the Isle of Man, the Channel Islands or abroad. Everything is posted to you.

## What are the options?

| Option | Price | When it arrives |
|---|---|---|
| Soon | £3.95, free over £50 | 2 to 3 working days |
| Sooner | £6.95 | The next working day, if you order by 2pm, Monday to Friday |
| Cold | £8.95 | Sent Monday to Thursday. Arrives the next day before 1pm. Order by 12:00 |

Working days are Monday to Friday, not bank holidays.

You order a Blip on Monday at 10am and choose Sooner. It arrives on Tuesday.

## Which things go which way?

- **The Emergency Sandwich and Ice by Post:** Cold only. Each goes as its own parcel. If you order other things too, a cold thing and a warm thing arrive as two parcels: one cold, one warm. You pay for delivery once, at the Cold price.
- **Ice by Post:** we do not send it when the forecast at your address is 28°C or above. We hold the order and offer a new date or a refund.
- **The Pocket Jungle:** Sooner only, Monday to Thursday. We do not send it when the forecast is below 0°C. We hold the order and offer a new date or a refund.
- **The Boulder (Skimming Stones):** it is heavy, so Soon only.
- **Everything else:** Soon or Sooner.

## What if something is out of stock?

Items in stock are sent straight away. Items on pre-order or out of stock follow when we have them, with no extra delivery charge.

## How long can delivery take?

Whatever date we show you, we aim for it. The law says no more than 30 days from your order, unless we agreed a later date with you, such as a pre-order.

## What if it is late?

If a parcel arrives more than 2 working days after its estimated date, ask and we refund the delivery charge. A support agent does this. If the date mattered and we miss it, or we refuse to deliver, you can end the order for a full refund. Otherwise you can give us a new deadline, and end the order if we miss that.

## What if it is lost?

After 5 working days we treat a parcel as lost. We send it again or refund it. You choose.

## Whose risk is it in the post?

Ours, until it is in your hands, or in the hands of someone you named. If it is lost or damaged on the way, you do not pay. See Damage and loss.

## Can I change delivery?

Until the order is packed, yes. After that, no. See Cancelling or changing an order.

## Will I get my delivery charge back if I send something back?

If you return the whole order, yes, up to the cost of Soon. See Returns and refunds.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
