# Terms of use

## The short version

These are the rules for buying from us. We describe things honestly, charge what we show, and fix what goes wrong. Nothing here takes away your legal rights.

## Who are we?

Overthought & Co. is a fictional company, so this site is a demonstration. Nothing here is really for sale, no payment is taken and nothing is sent. We write our policies as a careful real shop would. We have no shop to visit. Contact us through the website, 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Saturday. We aim to reply within 1 working day.

## What do I pay?

Prices are in pounds sterling. The total you see at checkout, with delivery, is the total you pay. Delivery is Soon (£3.95, free over £50), Sooner (£6.95) or Cold (£8.95). See Delivery.

## How can I pay?

Four ways: Card (Visa, Mastercard or American Express), Tap (the wallet on your phone), PayPost (log in to your PayPost account and approve) or Later (pay in 30 days). All are pretend: no real money moves.

Later is for orders from £10 to £250, not counting delivery. It is not for a bag of only fresh food that travels Cold, because that is eaten long before the payment is due. A quick check decides straight away. If it says yes, we send your order now and you pay within 30 days. If it says no, nothing is owed and you can pay another way.

If a payment does not go through, no money is taken. We hold the order for 24 hours so you can try again, then cancel it. Refunds go back the way you paid. On a Later order you have not paid yet, a refund is taken off what you owe instead.

## How do orders work?

You can cancel or change an order until it is packed. After that you can still change your mind and return it. We deliver to the UK mainland only. See Cancelling or changing an order.

## Can I change my mind?

Yes. You have 30 days after delivery for most items. The law gives 14. Food that goes off quickly cannot be returned. Some other things have limits. Postage for a change of mind is £2.95, taken off your refund. See Returns and refunds.

## What if it is faulty?

The law says goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If they are not, you can have a refund, repair or replacement. See Damage and loss. Whatever else we say, those rights stay.

## What warranty do I get?

12 months on most things. 24 months on Blip and Boombox Mini. 60 months on The Daypack. See Warranty. It is on top of your legal rights.

## How do you describe the products?

We describe our products with care. The page you buy from is part of your contract. If we get something wrong, we put it right. Nothing on this site is professional advice. The Indoor Helmet is a comfort object, not safety equipment. See Product safety.

## Allergies

Allergen information is on every food page before you buy and comes with your parcel. See Product safety.

## What about services?

Services such as Borrow Box and The Fix Club have their own rules. Some services are not on offer at the moment. Nothing you buy from us as a product depends on a service. See Bookings, Hire and borrowing, Service cancellation and Repairs.

## What if I am unhappy?

See Complaints. We tell you what we can do and what we cannot.

## What about my details?

See Privacy and Cookies and browser storage.

## What if these terms change?

We may update them. The version on the site is the current one. A change does not apply to an order you have already placed.

Nothing here limits your legal rights.
