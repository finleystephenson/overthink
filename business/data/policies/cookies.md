# Cookies and browser storage

## The short version

This site keeps a few things in your browser so the shop works. We use nothing for advertising or tracking. Overthought & Co. is a fictional company, so no real payments are taken and nothing is sent.

## What does the site store?

- **Your orders and bookings.** Saved in your own browser so you can see them again. They are not sent to us.
- **Your basket.** Items you add are remembered if you leave the page.
- **Your choices.** For example, your cookie choice.

## Why am I not asked to accept them?

The law says we must ask before storing things on your device, unless they are strictly needed for something you asked for. Your basket and orders are needed for the shop to work, so we do not ask. This covers cookies and similar tools, such as browser storage.

You buy a Spare Sock. The site remembers it is in your basket. That is needed, so there is no banner.

## What do we not use?

- Advertising cookies.
- Tracking you across other websites.
- Analytics that follow you from page to page.
- Anything from other companies that tracks you.

We do not sell your data. If we ever add anything that needs your consent, we will ask first, with a clear Yes and No. Carrying on browsing will not count as a Yes. You will be able to change your mind as easily as you said yes. This page would change first.

## The assistant

Conversations with the assistant may be stored. See Privacy.

## How long do things stay?

Until you clear them. We do not set an end date.

## How do I clear or block them?

Look under privacy or site data in your browser settings. If you clear them, your orders, bookings and basket disappear. If you block them, the shop may not work.

## What are the details?

The law here is the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, regulation 6. The Information Commissioner's Office explains it at ico.org.uk. Anything that identifies you is also covered by our Privacy page.
