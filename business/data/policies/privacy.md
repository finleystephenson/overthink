# Privacy

## The short version

Overthought & Co. is a fictional company, so this site is a demonstration. Orders you make here are stored in your own browser. No real payment is taken. Please do not enter real personal or payment details. Made-up ones work fine, such as an email ending @example.com.

## Who is responsible for your data?

Overthought & Co. We are the "controller" for anything we hold. Contact us through the website.

## What do we store, and why?

| What | Where | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders and bookings you make | Your own browser, on your own device | So you can see them again | Needed to run the shop service you asked for |
| Basket and choices, such as your cookie choice | Your own browser | So the shop works | Needed to run the shop service you asked for |
| Conversations with the assistant | May be stored | To answer you and improve the assistant | Our legitimate interest in running a support service |

Orders you make here are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them. No card details are taken, because no real payment is taken.

## Do you have to give us this?

You do not have to use the site. If you give no details, you cannot place an order.

## Who sees it?

Nobody outside your own device sees your orders. We do not sell personal data. We do not send marketing email from this site. A real shop would share your address with a courier and your payment with a card processor. This site shares nothing with either.

## Does anything go abroad?

Not from your browser storage. We have not set out where assistant conversations are processed, so do not tell the assistant anything you would not want kept.

## How long do we keep it?

- Browser storage: until you clear it.
- Assistant conversations: we have not set a time, so we cannot state one. A real shop would state one here.

## Do you make automated decisions about me?

No. The assistant can suggest answers, but anything it cannot decide goes to a person.

## What are my rights?

You can:

- ask to see what we hold
- ask us to correct it
- ask us to delete it
- ask us to stop using it, or to restrict it
- object to how we use it
- ask for a copy in a usable form
- withdraw consent where we asked for it, at any time

You can delete your orders and bookings by clearing this site's data in your browser. For anything kept from assistant conversations, ask us through the website. We answer within one month.

## Who can I complain to?

Us first, through the website. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

## Changes

We may update this page. The version on the site is the current one.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
