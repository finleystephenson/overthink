# Warranty

## The short version

Our warranty is a promise we make for free, on top of the law. It does not replace your legal rights and it does not shorten them.

## How long does it last?

| Products | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Most products | 12 months |
| Blip and Boombox Mini | 24 months |
| The Daypack | 60 months |
| Food, live plants, paper and card | None |

It starts on the day your order is delivered.

You bought a Blip on 3 March. It arrived on 5 March. The warranty runs to 5 March two years later.

## What does it cover?

Faults in the materials or the making, when you use the product normally.

## What does it not cover?

- Accidental damage, such as drops and liquid.
- Normal wear, including a battery fading to 80%.
- Loss or theft.
- Repairs or changes made by anyone other than The Fix Club.

These limits apply to our warranty only. They do not take away your legal rights. If a product was faulty when it arrived, the law may still help you, whoever has opened it.

## What do I need?

Proof of purchase: an order number or a product registration. We can register a product for you.

## What do we do?

We repair it first. If we cannot, we replace it. If we can do neither, we refund it. It is free, with a free label both ways. See Repairs.

## What about live plants?

The Pocket Jungle's plant has a 14-day health guarantee instead. If it is unhealthy within 14 days of delivery, we send a new plant free. Send a photo within 14 days. The glass has the usual 12 months.

## What is the law, then?

Whatever our warranty says, the law says goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If they are not, you can:

- reject them for a refund within 30 days
- ask for a free repair or replacement
- have some money back, or reject them, if that does not work

If a fault shows up in the first 6 months, we have to prove it was not there from the start. You have up to 6 years (5 in Scotland) to make a legal claim.

## Is a warranty claim the same as a return?

No. If you changed your mind, see Returns and refunds. The 30 days to change your mind are separate from the warranty.

## What happens after it ends?

The Fix Club can still repair some products for a fee. See Repairs.

## How do I claim?

Contact customer support with your order number and a description of the fault. We may ask for photos.

This warranty is on top of your legal rights. It does not affect them.
