# Damage and loss

## The short version

If something is broken, wrong or missing, tell us. We fix it for free. Our fast route is for the first 14 days. The law gives you more than that.

## What does the law say about faulty goods?

Goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for what you told us you wanted them for, and as we described them. If not:

- **First 30 days:** you can reject the item and get a full refund.
- **Or:** you can ask for a repair or a replacement, free. You choose which, unless it costs us far more than the other.
- **If that fails:** after one repair or replacement that does not work, you can have some money back, or reject it for a refund.
- **First 6 months:** we have to prove it was not faulty when it arrived.
- **Up to 6 years (5 in Scotland):** the time you have to make a legal claim. This does not mean a product must last that long.

Food that goes off quickly has a shorter rejection period, as long as it would reasonably last.

## What is our fast route?

Tell us within 14 days of delivery. For food, tell us within 24 hours. These are the times our quick fix works. If it is later, still tell us. The law still applies.

- **Damaged:** send a photo. You get a replacement or a refund. You choose. You only need to send it back if it cost more than £60.
- **Wrong item:** we send the right one free. We give you a free label for the wrong one.
- **Missing item:** first check whether your order came in two parcels. Cold items always go alone. If it is truly missing, we send it again or refund it.
- **Spoiled sandwich or Biscuit Tin:** send a photo within 24 hours of delivery. You get a full refund or a new one. If the whole order spoiled, we refund the Cold delivery charge too.

You get a Trophy on Monday. It has a dent. On Wednesday you send a photo. We refund the £19 or send a new one. It is your choice.

## Is melted Ice by Post a fault?

We say on the page that we cannot promise it is still frozen. Melting alone is not a fault. A torn bag, a leaking parcel or the wrong item is a fault. Tell us within 24 hours with a photo.

## Whose fault is damage in the post?

Ours. Goods are at our risk until they reach you. You never pay for a parcel lost or damaged in transit.

## When do I get my money?

Usually within 5 working days. The law says no later than 14 days after we agree you are owed it. It goes to the payment method you used.

## What is not covered by the fast route?

Damage you did after it arrived, such as drops. See Warranty. Hired items have their own rules in Hire and borrowing.
