# Service cancellation

This page covers Someone to Queue, Someone to Hold and Someone to Wait In. Services are not on offer for now.

## The short version

Cancel more than 24 hours before and you get everything back. Cancel later and you do not. If we cancel, you get everything back.

## If I cancel, what do I get?

| When you cancel | What you get |
|---|---|
| More than 24 hours before | Full refund |
| 24 hours or less before | No refund |

Refunds go to the payment method you used, within 5 working days.

You book Someone to Queue for Saturday at 10:00. You cancel on Thursday at 17:00. You get a full refund. If you cancel on Friday at 11:00, you do not.

## What does the law add?

For some services booked online, you may have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of booking. If a booking is for a set date and time, this right may not apply once the date is close. Where the law gives you more than this page, the law applies. We cannot take it away.

## Can I move my booking?

Yes. Moving to another slot is free up to 24 hours before, if the new slot is free.

## If we cancel

You get a full refund or a free move to another slot. You choose.

## If you do not show up

If you are not there and did not cancel, there is no refund.

## If the service is not done properly

Services must be done with reasonable care and skill. If not, tell us. We do it again or give some money back.

## Questions and exceptions

A coordinator can cancel a booking and refund it. Only the Support Lead can agree an exception to this page.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
