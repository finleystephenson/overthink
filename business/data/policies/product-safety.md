# Product safety

## The short version

We only sell things we believe are safe to use as described. We tell you the risks we know about. We do not claim any safety certificate, rating or lab test for any product.

## Is The Indoor Helmet safety equipment?

No. It is a soft padded cap for cupboard doors and low beams. It is not protective equipment and has not been tested to any helmet standard. Do not use it for cycling, climbing, sport or work. It will not protect you from a serious knock.

## How do I find out what is in the food?

Allergen information is on each food product page before you buy it. We also put it in the parcel. We list all 14 allergens that the law names, where they are in the product.

- **The Emergency Sandwich:** every sandwich contains wheat (gluten) and milk. Ham Salad also contains mustard. Egg & Cress also contains egg. Cheese & Tomato also contains cheese.
- **The Biscuit Tin:** contains wheat (gluten) and milk. Made in a kitchen that also handles nuts, egg and soy.
- **Ice by Post:** plain ice. No dry ice.

The sandwich kitchen also handles egg, mustard, soya, sesame, nuts and fish. We cannot promise any food is free of traces. If you have an allergy, contact customer support before you order.

## What do the dates mean?

The Emergency Sandwich has a use-by date. That date is about safety. Do not eat it after that date. Use it within 48 hours of dispatch. Do not eat it if the case is open or swollen. The Biscuit Tin has a best-before date. That is about quality. Biscuits are usually still safe after it, but may not be at their best.

## What about electronics and batteries?

Use the cable in the box. Stop using a device that is hot, swollen or damaged, unplug it and contact customer support. Blip and Boombox Mini charge over USB-C. Loud sound can harm your hearing.

## Small parts and sharp edges

Skimming stones and other small items can be a choking risk for young children. Keep them away from small children. Throw stones only at open water, with nobody in the way.

## Live plants and glass

Pocket Jungle plants are alive. Do not eat any part. The glass can break. Do not leave it in a sunny window, as glass can focus sunlight.

## What if I think something is unsafe?

Contact customer support straight away. Say what happened. Stop using the item. A person looks at every safety report. If something we sold turns out to be unsafe, we stop selling it and tell the people who bought it.

You bought a Boombox Mini. The battery swells. Stop using it. Unplug it. Contact us. We arrange a free replacement or a refund.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
