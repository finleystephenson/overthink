# Cancelling or changing an order

This page is about product orders. Services have their own rules. See Bookings and Service cancellation.

## The short version

Until we pack your order, you can cancel it or change it. After we pack it, you can still change your mind. It just becomes a return.

## Can I cancel?

Yes. Until the order is packed, cancel it and you get everything back, including delivery. Refunds go to the payment method you used, within 5 working days.

## What if it is already packed?

You can still cancel. The law lets you cancel a delivery order before it arrives and for 14 days after. Tell us. When the parcel arrives, send it back with the label we email you. This counts as a return, so the £2.95 label charge applies. See Returns and refunds.

You ordered a Daypack on Monday. We packed it on Tuesday. On Wednesday you change your mind. Tell us on Wednesday. Send it back once it arrives. You have 30 days from the day it arrives.

## What cannot be cancelled?

Food goes off quickly, so the law does not give a right to cancel The Emergency Sandwich or Ice by Post. Until they are packed, we cancel them for free anyway. Once packed, they cannot be cancelled.

## What can I change?

Until the order is packed you can change:

- the delivery address
- the delivery method
- the choice, such as the colour, of the same product at the same price

Nothing else can be changed. To get something different, cancel and place a new order. Once packed, nothing can be changed.

## Can I remove one item?

Yes, if the order is not packed. Ask a support agent. The assistant can cancel a whole order only.

## What about orders in two parcels?

Some items travel on their own, such as chilled food. An unpacked parcel can still be cancelled or changed. A packed parcel cannot be changed.

## What if my payment fails?

Nothing is packed. You can try again with another card. We hold the order for 24 hours, then cancel it. We do not keep the stock for you while it waits.

## Pre-orders

You pay when you order. You can cancel for a full refund any time before it ships. Accessories ordered with it ship straight away. After it arrives, the usual 30 days apply.

## If we cannot deliver

If we refuse or fail to deliver, or we miss a date you told us mattered, you can end the order and get a full refund. We refund without delay.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
