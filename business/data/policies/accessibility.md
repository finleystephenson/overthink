# Accessibility

## The short version

We want everyone to be able to use the site and order. Nobody has checked the site against any accessibility standard yet, so we do not say it meets one. If something does not work for you, tell us and we will find another way.

## What do we aim for?

We try to follow common web accessibility guidance (WCAG). We have not had an audit and we claim no level.

## Can I order without the website?

You do not have to use the website to get help.

- Ask us through the website, 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Saturday. We aim to reply within 1 working day.
- Customer support can place an order for you. Give us the details.

We have no shop to visit, so everything is done online and by post.

You cannot use the checkout with a screen reader. Ask us through the website. We place the order for you.

## What about delivery?

If you cannot get to your door, change the delivery address before the order is packed. See Delivery. If a parcel is hard to open or carry, tell us. We may be able to help next time.

## What about services?

Services such as Borrow Box are not on offer at the moment. When they come back, we will say honestly what access is possible, for example step-free entry, seating or a hearing loop. Please ask before you book and tell us what you need.

## Can I have things in another format?

Policies and help answers are plain text. If you need one in another format, such as large print, ask and we will do our best.

## What is a reasonable adjustment?

The Equality Act 2010 asks a business to make reasonable changes so disabled people are not at a disadvantage. It is not a promise to do everything. Tell us what would help. We will say what we can do, and if we cannot do it, why.

## How do I tell you about a barrier?

Tell customer support what you could not use, which page it was on and what device or help tool you used. We reply within 1 working day. If you are not happy with the answer, see Complaints.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
