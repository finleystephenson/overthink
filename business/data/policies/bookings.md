# Bookings

## The short version

Services are not on offer for now. This page says how they work when they are. Nothing you buy as a product depends on a service.

## Where do services happen?

At your address, or within 30 miles of us. Someone to Clap can go up to 60 miles, for a charge per mile.

## Borrow Box

Borrow items for a weekend or a week. Book at least 24 hours ahead. You can borrow up to 3 items per booking. See Hire and borrowing.

## Someone to Clap

A small crew that applauds you.

- Times: 10:00, 13:00, 16:00 and 19:00, Monday to Saturday.
- Two crews work each time. Book at least 72 hours ahead.
- Up to 30 miles is included. After that it is £1 a mile, up to 60 miles.
- You must give the exact address, the name of the person to be clapped and when the clapping should start.

We will not clap at funerals, court hearings, exams or medical appointments. We will not boo, heckle or clap sarcastically. We will not clap for someone who has not agreed. We do not go to places that need tickets you have not bought for the crew.

## Someone to Queue

£9 for each 30 minutes. Minimum 1 hour, maximum 6 hours. Start times are on the hour from 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Saturday. Book at least 24 hours ahead. We cannot do the bit where you are served. We will not queue for anything that needs ID in your name. Tell us the place, what the queue is for and when you will arrive.

## Someone to Hold

£6 a call, with up to 60 minutes on hold. Extra time is £3 for each 30 minutes. Book at least 24 hours ahead. Give us the number and what the call is about. We never give your details or pretend to be you. We do not hold for emergency, medical or bank security lines.

## Someone to Wait In

Half a day (4 hours) is £45. A full day (8 to 6) is £80. Book at least 24 hours ahead. A named waiter with ID, told to you in advance, signs for deliveries. They do not let anyone else in and do not make decisions about repairs.

## What if I change or cancel?

- **Someone to Clap:** full refund up to 48 hours before, half up to 24 hours before, none after. Changing the time is free once, up to 48 hours before. A second change costs £15.
- **Queue, Hold and Wait In:** see Service cancellation.
- **Borrow Box:** see Hire and borrowing.

To change a booking, ask customer support with the booking number and the email or postcode on it.

## What does the law add?

Services must be done with reasonable care and skill. If one is not, you can ask for it to be done again or for some money back. You may also have a legal right to cancel a service booked online within 14 days. This does not apply to every service. Where it does, the law applies.

## Availability

A coordinator runs the bookings. We can only promise a place the booking page shows as free.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
