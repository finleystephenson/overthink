# Returns and refunds

## The short version

You can change your mind and send most things back for 30 days after they arrive. If something is faulty, the law gives you strong rights on top. Either way you get your money back to the card you used.

## Can I change my mind?

Yes. The law gives you 14 days. We give you 30 days. The days start on the day your order is delivered.

You bought a Boombox Mini on 3 March. It arrived on 5 March. You have until 4 April to tell us and send it back.

## How do I send something back?

Use your order page or ask us through the website. We email a prepaid label. Pack the item with everything that came with it. We do not swap items. Return it for a refund and place a new order.

## Who pays to send it back?

If you changed your mind, we take £2.95 off your refund to cover the label. If it was faulty, damaged or the wrong item, returning it is free.

## When do I get my money?

We refund within 5 working days of the parcel arriving. The law says no later than 14 days after we get it back, or after you show proof of posting, whichever is sooner. The money goes back to the payment method you used.

## What do I get back?

You get the price of the item. You also get the delivery charge back if you return the whole order, or if the item was faulty, damaged or wrong. If you chose a faster delivery, the law only makes us refund the standard price (Soon). We refund the lot anyway if the item was faulty.

You bought a Spare Sock for £7 and paid £3.95 for Soon delivery. You change your mind and return the whole order. You get £7 + £3.95 − £2.95 = £8.00.

You bought a Blip for £149 with free delivery. It is returned unused. You get £149 − £2.95 = £146.05.

## What condition must it be in?

For the first 14 days, handle it as you would in a shop. You can switch on a Blip or a Boombox Mini and try it. Reset it to factory settings and take your account off before you send it. If you used it more than a shop would allow, we may take off what it lost in value. We will explain the amount in writing. We will not refuse the refund altogether.

From day 15 to day 30, which is our extra time, it must be unused, complete and in its original packaging.

## What cannot be sent back to change your mind?

The law lets us say no to these:

- The Emergency Sandwich and Ice by Post. They go off quickly.
- The Biscuit Tin once opened. It is sealed food.
- The Indoor Helmet once its hygiene seal is broken.
- Live plants once delivered. We treat them as perishable.

Some things we do not take back after day 14: worn Spare Socks and thrown Skimming Stones. Before day 14, we may take off the value they lost.

## What if it is faulty?

The law, not us, sets these rules. See Damage and loss and Warranty. Faulty items can always be sent back, free, even if they are on the list above.

## What if it fails our check?

We never keep your money and your item. If it comes back damaged or incomplete, we tell you what is wrong and what we take off. You can ask for a stage 1 review. See Complaints.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
