# Hire and borrowing (Borrow Box)

## The short version

Borrow Box lets you borrow things for a short time. You pay a deposit, we deliver and collect, and you get the deposit back when the item returns in good shape. Services are not on offer for now.

## How long can I borrow?

- **Weekend:** from Friday 10:00 to Monday 12:00.
- **Week:** any 7 days, from 10:00 on day one to 12:00 on day 7.

Book at least 24 hours ahead. You can borrow up to 3 items in one booking. Each item shows its price and deposit on its page.

## How does the item get to me?

We deliver and pick up within 30 miles of us, for £12 each way. Everything is delivered to you.

## What is the deposit?

Each item has a deposit. We take it when you book. We refund it within 3 working days of the item coming back clean, complete and working. The law says refunds are made without delay and in any case within 14 days.

You borrow a speaker for a weekend. You return it on Monday at 11:00, clean and working. Your deposit is back by Thursday.

## What if I return it late?

A late return costs £10 a day. Each day or part of a day after the deadline counts as one day. The fee comes out of your deposit first. It cannot be more than the item's replacement value.

## What if I cancel?

- Up to 48 hours before: full refund.
- After that: 50% back.
- The deposit is always refunded in full.

You may also have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of booking. It does not apply to everything booked for a set date. Ask us if you are unsure.

## Can I change dates?

Free, up to 48 hours before, if the item is free on the new dates.

## What if it is damaged?

Normal wear is fine. If an item is damaged or a part is missing, we charge the repair cost, up to the item's replacement value. We take it from your deposit first. A coordinator decides the amount. If you disagree, ask for a person. See Complaints.

## What if the item does not work?

Hired items must work as described and be in good order. If one does not, tell us at once. We replace it, or refund the hire.

## What do we ask of you?

Use it as it is meant to be used. Keep it dry unless it is meant to be wet. Return it with all its parts. Do not lend it on or sell it.

## Questions

A coordinator runs the bookings. Customer support can help with anything this page does not cover.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
