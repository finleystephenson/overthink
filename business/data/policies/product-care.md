# Product care

## The short version

This page gives general advice. Each product page has its own care notes. Those come first. Looking after things properly keeps them working. It does not change your legal rights.

## How do I look after chilled food?

The Emergency Sandwich is made to order. Keep it in the fridge. Eat it within 48 hours of dispatch. Ice by Post comes in a wool-insulated box with gel packs and no dry ice. Put the ice in the freezer soon after it arrives. We cannot promise it is still frozen, so look at it as soon as you open the box.

## How do I look after a Pocket Jungle?

The plant needs bright, indirect light and a light mist now and then. Check it in the first few days. If it looks unhealthy within 14 days of delivery, send a photo and we send a new plant free. Keep the glass out of a south-facing window.

## How do I look after Blip and Boombox Mini?

They charge over USB-C. Keep them dry. They resist splashes but are not waterproof. Batteries fade with use. Falling to 80% of the original capacity is normal wear. The Fix Club can replace a battery for a fixed price. See Repairs.

## How do I wash The Daypack, The Spare Sock and The Indoor Helmet?

Wash them as the label says.

- If you may want to send The Indoor Helmet back, leave its hygiene seal on until you are sure. Once the seal is broken we cannot take it back to change your mind.
- Keep The Spare Sock unworn until you are sure.

## How do I look after Skimming Stones and The Starter Puddle?

Slate stones can chip when thrown. That is normal. The Starter Puddle holds 15 mm of water. Empty it before a frost.

## How do I look after paper things?

The Paper Aeroplane is best kept dry and flat.

## What counts as wear, and what is a fault?

Wear is what happens with normal use, such as a battery fading or a heel thinning. A fault is a product that was not made properly, such as a seam that opens early. The law says goods must be durable for a reasonable time. If you think something has failed too soon, you may have a fault claim. See Damage and loss. You can ask us even if we call it wear.

You have had The Daypack for 14 months. A seam splits. It has a 60-month warranty. We repair it free.

## When something goes wrong

Check your warranty first. The Fix Club can repair some products. Accidental damage is not covered by the warranty, but may be repairable for a fee.

## Keep your proof of purchase

For a warranty claim you need an order number or a product registration. Keep your order email.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
