# The Fix Club (repairs)

## The short version

The Fix Club repairs the things we sell, by post. If your item is faulty, the repair is free. If you broke it, there is a fee. You never need to visit anywhere.

## What does The Fix Club repair?

Blip, Boombox Mini, The Daypack, The Indoor Helmet, The Starter Puddle, The Other Umbrella and The Trophy. Nothing else. Food is not covered.

## What does it cost?

| Product | Repair | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Blip | Screen | £49 |
| Blip | Battery | £29 |
| Boombox Mini | Battery | £35 |
| The Daypack | Zip | £25 |
| The Daypack | Buckle fitted | £8 |

Some repairs have no fixed price: Indoor Helmet re-stitching, Starter Puddle chip and crack repair, Trophy re-plating and anything on The Other Umbrella. A technician quotes after looking at the item. We do not guess prices.

## Is it free if the item is faulty?

Yes, in two cases.

- **In warranty:** if our warranty covers the fault, the repair is free, with a free label both ways. See Warranty.
- **By law:** if the item was not as it should be when it arrived, the law says a repair or replacement is free, whatever our warranty says. You do not pay postage or an assessment fee.

You bought a Boombox Mini 8 months ago. The battery will not charge. It is in warranty (24 months). We repair it free.

## What if I broke it?

Out of warranty, or damage not covered, there is a £15 assessment fee. We take it when the item arrives. If you accept the quote, it comes off the repair price. If you decline, we keep it. If we cannot repair the item, we refund it. You pay £4.95 to send the item in. Sending it back is included in the repair price.

## How does it work?

1. You ask for a repair.
2. You send the item.
3. A technician looks at it and we send a quote. It is valid for 14 days.
4. You accept or decline.
5. If you accept, we repair it and post it back. If it cannot be repaired, we tell you.

## How long does it take?

We aim for 10 working days from the day the item arrives with us.

## Is the repair guaranteed?

Yes. The repair work has a 90-day guarantee. We do repairs with reasonable care and skill. If a repair is not done properly, tell us and we put it right.

## Before you send it

Remove your own data from the Blip. Pack the item well. Tell us what is wrong.

## What if I have changed my mind about a product?

That is a return, not a repair. See Returns and refunds.

This policy does not affect your legal rights.
